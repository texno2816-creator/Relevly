About the project

How it works

ResearchLens is a guide between large databases of research papers and people. It does not replace reading the papers, but it saves the time spent on choosing them: you see right away which works are worth opening.

Where the papers come from

From OpenAlex, an open database of hundreds of millions of research works. By default only open-access papers are shown; the “Include paywalled articles” switch adds the rest. If one paper is recorded in the database several times, we keep a single copy.

How the AI scores a paper

The model reads only the title and the abstract. First it splits your query into parts by importance:

  • main part: what you want to learn about (for example, “training a neural network”);
  • second part: the key object (for example, “board games”);
  • details: age, place, years and so on.

Then it gives a score from 1 to 10 and writes one sentence with the reason:

Fits{{ config.YES_THRESHOLD }}+from {{ config.YES_THRESHOLD }} to 10both the main and the second parts are present; matching details raise the score
Partly{{ config.UNCLEAR_THRESHOLD }}+from {{ config.UNCLEAR_THRESHOLD }} to {{ (config.YES_THRESHOLD - 0.1) | round(1) }}the main part is present, but the second part is missing or only loosely related
No fit<{{ config.UNCLEAR_THRESHOLD }}below {{ config.UNCLEAR_THRESHOLD }}the main part is missing; such papers are hidden

The model also labels the type of work: a review, a study with data, or a method/theory. The category is calculated from the score by the program, not by the AI, so it always matches the score.

Papers without an abstract

If a paper has no abstract, it cannot be scored fairly. Such papers are not sent to the AI; it reads the next ones in the results instead, and these papers are listed separately at the bottom so you can check them yourself.

Model

The site uses {{ config.LLM_MODEL }}. It was chosen after comparing three models on 60 papers rated by hand: it had the best accuracy, the fewest unfitting papers in the results and the highest speed.

Limitations

  • The AI sees only the abstract, not the whole paper, and can make mistakes, especially on very narrow or vague queries.
  • The score of the same paper can change slightly from one search to another.
  • Paper topics are assigned by OpenAlex, not by our AI, so they are sometimes inaccurate.

Data

Queries and search results are stored on the server to improve search quality. Projects are stored only in your browser.
